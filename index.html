Political Science · International Relations & Political Methodology

How a Western-centric world is made, reproduced, and challenged. 一个西方中心的世界，如何被造就、再生产与挑战。

I study the Western-centric international hierarchy and its racial-colonial substructure — tracing how it is produced through colonial histories, reproduced in the everyday attitudes of Global South publics, and challenged by rising outsider states. 我研究西方中心的国际等级及其种族—殖民底层结构：它如何在殖民历史中被造就，如何在全球南方公众的日常态度中被再生产，又如何被崛起的“外来国”所挑战。

The Research Program

One hierarchy, three agendas — genesis, reproduction, renegotiation. 一套等级，三个议程——生成、再生产、再协商。

The three agendas are mutually supporting and cross-linked; each carries completed or under-review work. 三个议程相互支撑、彼此交叉，每个都已有完成或在审的核心成果。
