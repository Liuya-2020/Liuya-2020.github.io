Political Science · International Relations & Political Methodology

How a Western-centric order is made, reproduced, and challenged. 一个西方中心的秩序，如何被造就、再生产与挑战。

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Political Science (International Relations and Political Methodology) at The Ohio State University (OSU), finishing in May 2027. My dissertation, a book project titled In the Shadows of Imperialism, asks why formerly colonized states develop durable yet divergent foreign policy orientations toward their former rulers. 我是俄亥俄州立大学（OSU）政治学博士候选人（国际关系与政治方法方向），预计 2027 年 5 月毕业。我的博士论文是一个书稿项目，题为《帝国阴影之下》（In the Shadows of Imperialism），探讨：为何曾被殖民的国家会对前宗主国形成持久而分化的外交政策取向。

I study the life cycle of imperial hierarchy after formal sovereignty: its genesis, reproduction, and contestation. I approach it with comparative-historical analysis, process tracing, statistical and survey analysis, and computational text analysis, drawing on sources in Chinese, English, and Korean. My cases center on East and Southeast Asia. 我研究形式主权之后帝国等级的生命周期：它的生成、再生产与挑战。我综合运用比较历史分析、过程追踪、统计与调查分析与计算文本分析，并使用中文、英文和韩文材料。我的案例以东亚和东南亚为中心。

A sole-authored paper on ethnic status is under review, and an earlier version won the department’s Madison Scott Award (2024). A coauthored article on China’s recognition dilemma, of which I am first and corresponding author, is at the revise-and-resubmit stage. My work has been supported by OSU’s Mershon Center for International Security Studies and the National Science Foundation. 我独立撰写的族群地位论文正在审稿中，其早期版本获政治学系 Madison Scott 奖（2024）。与他人合著的关于中国承认困境的论文（我为第一作者兼通讯作者）已进入修改后再投阶段。我的研究获得 OSU Mershon 国际安全研究中心和美国国家科学基金会的支持。

I hold M.A. degrees from UC San Diego and Seoul National University and a B.A. from Fudan University. I am also a Research Fellow of Bear F. Braumoeller’s Modeling Emergent Social Order (MESO) Lab. 我拥有加州大学圣迭戈分校和首尔大学的硕士学位，以及复旦大学学士学位。我同时是 Bear F. Braumoeller 创立的“Modeling Emergent Social Order”（MESO）实验室的研究员。
