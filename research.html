Imperial Legacies & Postcolonial National Habitus帝国遗产与后殖民民族惯习

How do imperial modes of governing difference and the fate of imperial authority at independence shape postcolonial national identity narratives and foreign policy orientations toward former rulers? I argue that these orientations are rooted in postcolonial national habitus: historically formed, socially shared dispositions through which certain national identities and foreign policy pathways come to feel legitimate and thinkable. The dissertation develops four types of habitus and examines them through cross-national analysis of 149 former colony–metropole dyads and comparative-historical cases in East and Southeast Asia. 帝国治理差异的方式与独立时帝国权威的归宿，如何塑造后殖民国家的民族身份叙事及其对前宗主国的外交政策取向？我认为这些取向根植于后殖民民族惯习：由历史形成、社会共享的性情倾向，使特定的民族身份与外交政策路径显得正当且“可思”。博士论文提出四种类型的惯习，并通过 149 组前殖民地—宗主国配对的跨国分析，以及东亚和东南亚的比较历史案例加以检验。

In the Shadows of Imperialism: Postcolonial National Habitus, Identity, and Foreign Policy Orientation

Dissertation / book project博士论文 / 书稿项目Ph.D. expected May 2027预计 2027 年 5 月获得博士学位

Abstract摘要

Why do countries emerging from imperial rule develop divergent and durable foreign policy orientations toward former imperial powers? I argue that the variation is rooted in postcolonial national habitus: historically formed, socially distributed dispositions through which national identity and foreign policy orientation are enacted as one tacit, relational practical sense. Two historical conditions shape it: whether empire homogenized or differentiated the societies it ruled, and whether imperial authority was inherited or repudiated at independence. Together they generate four configurations: Mimicry/Deference, Victimhood/Defiance, Hybrid/Engagement, and Autonomist/Buffering. The dissertation combines cross-national analysis of 149 former colony–metropole dyads with comparative-historical cases in East and Southeast Asia, centered on Philippines–U.S., Korea–Japan, and Indonesia–Netherlands. It shows that imperial hierarchy persists after sovereignty by shaping which foreign policy choices appear thinkable, appropriate, and desirable, beyond material dependence, institutional constraint, or elite interests.为何走出帝国统治的国家会对前宗主国形成分化而持久的外交政策取向？我认为这一差异根植于后殖民民族惯习：由历史形成、社会共享的性情倾向，使民族身份与外交政策取向成为同一种隐性、关系性实践感的两种表达。两个历史条件塑造它：帝国是同质化还是差异化地统治被殖民社会，以及独立时帝国权威是被继承还是被拒斥。二者共同产生四种类型：Mimicry/Deference、Victimhood/Defiance、Hybrid/Engagement 与 Autonomist/Buffering。论文将 149 组前殖民地—宗主国配对的跨国分析与东亚和东南亚的比较历史案例相结合，以菲律宾—美国、韩国—日本、印尼—荷兰为核心。研究表明，帝国等级在主权独立后之所以延续，是因为它塑造了哪些外交政策选择显得可想、恰当且值得向往，这超出了物质依赖、制度约束或精英利益所能解释的范围。

Why Postimperial Alignments Recover: Imperial Trust and the Resilience of the U.S.–Philippine Alliance

In preparation · submission Fall 2026写作中 · 计划 2026 年秋投稿

Imperial trust explains why the U.S.–Philippine alliance recovers after elite-led ruptures, traced through the Philippine Senate’s 1991 bases vote and Duterte’s attempted reorientation after 2016.帝国信任解释了为何美菲联盟在精英主导的断裂之后得以恢复；以 1991 年菲律宾参议院的基地条约表决和 2016 年后杜特尔特的重新定位为案例。

Abstract摘要

Why do inherited postimperial alignments recover after elite-led rupture attempts? Existing accounts emphasize material incentives, external threats, alliance institutions, and elite bargains, leaving underexplained why cooperation with a former metropole retains credibility across state and societal sites. I theorize imperial trust as a socially distributed, tacit, and affective disposition that renders inherited metropolitan standards of competence, authority, and worth familiar and credible after formal sovereignty. Imperial trust is most likely to form when homogenizing imperial rule is followed by political continuity at independence and is subsequently reproduced through institutional embedding and everyday embodiment. Using longitudinal process tracing of U.S.–Philippine relations, I trace imperial trust from its formation under colonial rule through its postindependence reproduction and effects during two rupture episodes: the 1991 rejection of U.S. bases and Duterte’s post-2016 reorientation toward China. The 1991 rupture ended permanent basing while preserving the wider alliance; Duterte challenged American authority more broadly but produced shallower institutional change. In both episodes, U.S.-linked expertise and protection retained professional and public credibility, allowing cooperation to recover in modified forms. By theorizing imperial trust, the article contributes to IR scholarship on hierarchy, trust, and postcolonial politics, showing how imperial hierarchy is reproduced from below after formal sovereignty, despite postcolonial elites’ attempts to break free.为何继承而来的后帝国联盟在精英主导的断裂尝试之后会恢复？现有解释强调物质激励、外部威胁、联盟制度与精英交易，却难以说明为何与前宗主国的合作在国家与社会各层面仍保有可信度。我将帝国信任理论化为一种社会性分布、隐性且带有情感的倾向，使继承自宗主国的能力、权威与价值标准在形式主权之后仍显得熟悉而可信。当同质化的帝国统治之后在独立时保持政治连续性，并经由制度嵌入与日常身体化不断再生产时，帝国信任最可能形成。我运用对美菲关系的纵向过程追踪，考察帝国信任从殖民统治下形成，到独立后再生产，再到两次断裂事件中发挥作用：1991 年拒绝美军基地条约，以及杜特尔特 2016 年后转向中国。1991 年的断裂终结了永久驻军，但保留了更广泛的联盟；杜特尔特更广泛地挑战了美国的权威，却只带来较浅的制度变化。两次事件中，与美国相关的专业知识与保护都保有职业界和公众的信任，使合作以调整后的形式得以恢复。通过理论化帝国信任，本文推进了国际关系学界关于等级、信任与后殖民政治的研究，表明形式主权之后，帝国等级如何自下而上地被再生产，即便后殖民精英试图挣脱。

Neither Bloc nor Break: Boundary Awareness and Non-Alignment in Postcolonial Multiethnic States

In preparation写作中

A theory of how ethnic groups that disagree about outside powers still reach a shared practical sense of limits, boundary awareness, that lets the state engage several powers without joining a bloc or splitting apart; developed from Indonesia and Malaysia.关于对外部大国意见分歧的族群如何仍能形成对限度的共享实践感——边界意识——使国家得以同多个大国往来，而不加入任何阵营，也不陷入分裂的理论；以印度尼西亚和马来西亚为起点。

Abstract摘要

Why do postcolonial regimes that depend most on a patron decline to bind themselves to it? Existing accounts trace non-alignment and hedging to rulers: regime survival or strategies of legitimation. They explain why rulers lean toward a patron, not why the lean stays short of formal commitment even as rulers and their legitimation strategies change. I locate the answer below the state, in the everyday relations among ethnic groups. Where ethnic groups hold opposed dispositions toward the same outside power, their ordinary members meet in daily life, and repeated friction produces boundary awareness, a shared practical sense of how far any orientation can go. Because any commitment would alienate some group, non-commitment becomes the one orientation no group rules out, and rulers legitimate it for that reason. States therefore calibrate instead of commit. I develop the argument through Indonesia and Malaysia and ask whether it extends to other postcolonial multiethnic states. The argument recasts non-alignment as a social outcome, not only a strategic choice.为什么那些最依赖某一赞助国的后殖民政权，仍拒绝在正式层面与其绑定？现有解释把不结盟与对冲归因于统治者：政权生存或合法性策略。它们解释了统治者为何倾向某一赞助国，却没有解释为何这种倾向即使在统治者及其合法性策略更替之后，仍止步于正式承诺。我把答案置于国家之下，置于族群之间的日常关系之中。当不同族群对同一外部大国持相互对立的性情倾向时，其普通成员在日常生活中相遇，反复的摩擦催生边界意识——对任何取向之限度的共享实践感。由于任何承诺都会疏远某一群体，不承诺便成为没有任何群体排斥的唯一取向，统治者也正因此而将其合法化。因此，国家选择校准而非承诺。本文以印度尼西亚和马来西亚为起点展开论证，并追问它能否推广到其他后殖民多族群国家。这一论证把不结盟重新理解为一种社会结果，而不仅是一种战略选择。

Ethnic Status & Segmented Habitus族群地位与分割惯习

How does ethnic status, a group’s position in domestic ethnopolitical and global racial-colonial hierarchies, shape how citizens in postcolonial multiethnic societies view foreign powers that stand in different relations to the Western-centric hierarchy? These group-level orientations reproduce imperial hierarchy from below. Where groups within one state hold different dispositions toward the same outside power, I call this segmented habitus. I theorize ethnic status as a group’s position at the intersection of domestic political incorporation (core vs. non-core) and global racial-colonial proximity (European-linked vs. non-European). 在后殖民多族群社会中，族群地位——即群体在国内族群政治等级与全球种族—殖民等级中的位置——如何塑造公民对与西方中心等级关系各异的外部大国的看法？这些群体层面的取向自下而上地再生产帝国等级。当同一国家内的不同群体对同一外部大国持有不同的性情倾向时，我称之为分割惯习。我将族群地位理论化为群体在国内政治纳入（核心 / 非核心）与全球种族—殖民亲近度（欧洲关联 / 非欧洲）交叉处的位置。

See also “Neither Bloc nor Break” under agenda 01: where groups hold different dispositions toward the same outside power, segmented habitus also sets limits on how far a divided state can commit to any one power. 另见议程 01 中的 “Neither Bloc nor Break”：当不同群体对同一外部大国持不同性情倾向时，分割惯习也限定了分裂社会能在多大程度上向任何单一大国作出承诺。

Global Powers, Local Divides: Ethnic Status and Foreign Public Opinion Toward the U.S. and China in Postcolonial Multiethnic States

Under review在审

Ethnic status shapes how citizens view the U.S. relative to China, tested with harmonized survey data across 58 postcolonial multiethnic states. An earlier version won OSU Political Science’s Madison Scott Award for Best Graduate Student Paper on Race and Ethnic Politics (2024).族群地位塑造公民对美国与中国的相对看法，基于 58 个后殖民多族群国家的整合民调数据加以检验。该论文早期版本获俄亥俄州立大学政治学系 Madison Scott 奖（种族与族群政治方向最佳研究生论文，2024）。

Abstract摘要

Why do citizens within the same postcolonial multiethnic state differ in their relative evaluations of the U.S. and China? Existing accounts emphasize liberal-democratic affinity, material exposure, and transnational identity ties but less often examine how ethnic stratification conditions the political meaning of these factors. I theorize ethnic status as a social position with two intersecting dimensions: domestic political incorporation, distinguishing ethnic cores from non-cores by state access, and racial-colonial proximity, distinguishing European-linked from non-European groups by Western-centered recognition. Citizens evaluate these powers by assessing which is more likely to affirm or threaten their group’s domestic incorporation and international standing. Using harmonized surveys of 132,690 respondents in 58 postcolonial multiethnic states, merged with the Ethnic Power Relations dataset, I find that European cores are most U.S.-favorable, non-European cores more China-leaning, and non-cores more context-dependent. U.S.-China competition for foreign public support thus runs through domestic ethnic cleavages and racial-colonial legacies.同一后殖民多族群国家内的公民为何在对美国与中国的相对评价上存在差异？现有解释强调自由民主亲和、物质暴露与跨国身份联系，却较少考察族群分层如何塑造这些因素的政治意涵。我将族群地位理论化为具有两个交叉维度的社会位置：国内政治纳入（按国家权力的获取区分核心与非核心族群）与种族—殖民亲近度（按西方中心的承认区分欧洲关联与非欧洲族群）。公民通过判断哪个大国更可能肯定或威胁其群体的国内纳入与国际地位来评价两国。基于 58 个后殖民多族群国家 132,690 名受访者的整合调查，并与 Ethnic Power Relations 数据集合并，我发现欧洲关联的核心群体最亲美，非欧洲的核心群体更倾向中国，非核心群体则更取决于情境。因此，中美对外国公众支持的竞争贯穿国内族群裂痕与种族—殖民遗产。

Sovereignty for Whom? Foreign-Backed Projects and Ethnic Mobilization in Postcolonial Multiethnic States

Early stage早期阶段

Argues that foreign-backed projects turn contentious when ethnic groups, reading them through their status in domestic and global hierarchies, see them as reinforcing exclusion or threatening their land, autonomy, or recognition.论证外国支持的项目之所以引发争议，是因为各族群依据自身在国内与全球等级中的地位来解读这些项目，并认为其强化了排斥，或威胁到其土地、自治或承认。

Abstract摘要

The same foreign-backed dam, port, or mine is often defended by one ethnic group and resisted by another bearing similar costs. Existing studies ask whether foreign-backed projects raise protest or conflict on average, or whether Chinese and Western sponsors differ, but treat local populations as interchangeable, so they cannot say why one project splits groups. I argue that the split follows ethnic status: a group’s domestic political incorporation (core or non-core) combined with its place in the global racial-colonial hierarchy (European-linked or not). A foreign-backed project is a status event, because its sponsor signals whose position it confirms or threatens. Non-European groups excluded from the state should resist projects from either sponsor, while non-European cores should resist Chinese projects less than Western ones and European-linked cores should side with the Western sponsor. I match all Chinese and World Bank infrastructure and extractive projects, including those that provoked no protest, to the homelands of core and non-core groups across postcolonial Africa and Latin America, and test whether protest differs across group types under the same sponsor and across sponsors for the same group type. The article carries ethnic status from opinion to mobilization and moves the study of foreign influence from power projection to power reception.同一座外国支持的大坝、港口或矿山，常常被一个族群捍卫，又被承受相近成本的另一个族群抵制。现有研究或检验外国支持项目平均而言是否增加抗议与冲突，或比较中国与西方出资方的差异，却把当地人口视为同质，因而无法解释为何同一项目会使不同族群分裂。我论证这种分裂遵循族群地位：即群体的国内政治纳入（核心或非核心）与其在全球种族—殖民等级中的位置（欧洲关联或非欧洲）的结合。外国支持的项目是一种地位事件，因为出资方会向人们传递其认可或威胁了谁的位置。被排除在国家之外的非欧洲族群应抵制任一出资方的项目；非欧洲的核心族群对中国项目的抵制应弱于对西方项目的抵制；欧洲关联的核心族群则应站在西方出资方一边。我将中国与世界银行的全部基础设施和采掘类项目（包括未引发抗议的项目）与后殖民时期非洲和拉丁美洲核心与非核心族群的家园相匹配，检验在同一出资方之下不同群体类型之间、以及对同一群体类型而言不同出资方之间，抗议是否存在差异。本文将族群地位的分析从舆论推进到动员，并把对外国影响的研究从权力投射转向权力接收。

Rising Powers & Recognition崛起大国与承认

How do rising outsider states contest a Western-centric order while seeking recognition within it? I argue that they face a recognition dilemma: in a Western-centric hierarchy, material acceptance and equal symbolic standing cannot be secured together. I examine it through computational text analysis of top-leader foreign-policy discourse, beginning with China and extending to other rising outsider states. 崛起的“外来国”如何在寻求西方中心秩序承认的同时，对其提出挑战？我认为它们面临承认困境：在西方中心的等级中，物质接纳与平等的象征地位无法同时获得。我通过对最高领导人外交政策话语的计算文本分析来检验这一论点，从中国出发，并延伸到其他崛起的外来国。

China’s Recognition Dilemma: Development-Oriented Conformity, Nationalist Assertion, and Foreign Policy Discourse, 1978–2021

Coauthored with Haoming Xiong · first & corresponding author与 Haoming Xiong 合著 · 第一作者兼通讯作者Revise & resubmit · Chinese Journal of International Politics修改后再投 · Chinese Journal of International Politics

China’s shifts between development-oriented conformity and nationalist assertion recur as a response to a recognition dilemma, not a linear move from restraint to assertiveness, traced through computational text analysis of 354 top-leader speeches, 1978–2021.中国在发展导向的遵从与民族主义主张之间的摇摆，是对承认困境的反复回应，而非从克制到强硬的线性转变；依据对 354 篇最高领导人讲话（1978–2021）的计算文本分析。

Abstract摘要

Why do scholars often find it difficult to classify China’s approach to international order as either status quo or revisionist? We explain China’s foreign policy ambivalence by theorizing a recognition dilemma: China’s pursuit of material strength and symbolic equality as a great power is structurally constrained by its outsider position within a Western-centred hierarchy. Development-oriented conformity advances material acceptance but defers symbolic recognition to existing hierarchical standards, while nationalist assertion demands immediate symbolic equality but risks undermining external trust conducive to material acceptance. To trace this recognition dilemma, we analyze 354 top-leader speeches (1978–2021) using a context-sensitive, multi-measure text-as-data approach. We find a stable thematic core centred on development, sovereignty, equality and anti-hegemonism; temporal recalibration from a security-heavy register toward development-oriented themes after the Cold War, and further toward normative and order-shaping language from the late 1990s onward; and audience-sensitive rhetoric, with material and symbolic claims bound into national rejuvenation for domestic audience, readily fused into South-South solidarity toward fellow developing countries, and coexisting in tension toward the established core of developed countries. This article thus reinterprets China’s ambivalence as recurrent efforts to manage a historically constituted recognition dilemma rather than as pragmatic improvisation.为何学界常常难以将中国对国际秩序的态度归入现状国或修正主义国家？我们通过理论化“承认困境”来解释中国外交政策的矛盾性：中国作为大国追求物质实力与象征性平等，在西方中心的等级体系中因其“外来者”地位而受到结构性约束。发展导向的遵从推进物质上的接纳，却把象征性承认交由既有的等级标准来决定；民族主义主张则要求即刻的象征性平等，却可能损害有利于物质接纳的外部信任。为追踪这一承认困境，我们采用情境敏感的多指标文本即数据方法，分析 1978–2021 年间 354 篇最高领导人讲话。我们发现：稳定的主题核心围绕发展、主权、平等与反霸权；话语随时间调整，冷战后从偏重安全转向发展导向主题，并自 1990 年代后期起进一步转向规范性与塑造秩序的表述；话语还因受众而异——面向国内受众，物质与象征诉求被整合进民族复兴；面向发展中国家，二者自然融入南南团结；面向发达国家的既有核心，二者则在张力中并存。因此，本文将中国的矛盾性重新解读为对一种历史形成的承认困境的反复应对，而非务实的临机应变。

Recognition Dilemmas Beyond China

Early stage早期阶段

Extends the recognition-dilemma argument to rising outsider states beyond China, beginning with India, Turkey, and Brazil. Rather than treating them as interchangeable “rising powers,” it asks how distinct imperial, postcolonial, and regional histories shape their pursuit of material inclusion and symbolic equality.将承认困境的论点延伸到中国以外的崛起外来国，从印度、土耳其和巴西入手。它不把这些国家视为可互换的“崛起大国”，而是追问各自不同的帝国、后殖民与区域历史如何塑造其对物质纳入与象征平等的追求。
