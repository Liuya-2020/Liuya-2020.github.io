The life cycle of a hierarchy. 一套等级的生命周期。

A single question runs through my work: how is the Western-centric international hierarchy, and its racial-colonial substructure, made, reproduced, and challenged? Three mutually supporting agendas trace it across that life cycle, each with its own evidence and method. 一个问题贯穿我的研究：西方中心的国际等级及其种族—殖民底层结构，如何被造就、再生产与挑战？三个相互支撑的议程沿这一生命周期展开，各有自己的证据与方法。

Colonial Legacies & Postcolonial Foreign Policy殖民遗产与后殖民外交政策

Why do countries emerging from imperial rule develop such divergent — yet durable — foreign-policy trajectories toward their former empires? I argue the variation is rooted in a postcolonial national habitus: historically constituted, embodied dispositions through which national identity and foreign policy co-emerge. Two sequential conditions — the mode of imperial incorporation (assimilative vs. accommodative) and the mode of separation at independence (negotiated vs. violent) — combine into four ideal-typical paths. 为何脱离帝国统治的国家，会形成如此分化却又长期稳定的对前宗主国外交轨迹？我认为这一差异根植于后殖民民族惯习：一套由历史构成、具身化的性情倾向，民族身份与外交政策在其中共同生成。两个序列条件——帝国纳入方式（同化型 / 适应型）与独立分离方式（协商型 / 暴力型）——组合出四类理想型路径。

Mimicry / Deference · Victimhood / Defiance · Hybridity / Engagement · Autonomy / Buffering

Dual Ethnic Hierarchies & Global South Reception双重族群等级与全球南方接收

The hierarchy is reproduced not only in state histories but in the everyday attitudes of stratified publics. Citizens evaluate great powers from ethnic-status positions set by two intersecting hierarchies — domestic political incorporation (core vs. non-core) and colonial-symbolic recognition (European vs. non-European). 等级不仅在国家历史中、也在分层公众的日常态度中被再生产。公众从族群地位出发评价大国——这一地位由两个交叉等级设定：国内政治纳入（核心 / 非核心）与殖民—象征承认（欧洲 / 非欧洲）。

The theory paper develops global order logics — U.S. liberal individualism vs. Chinese statist pluralism, two competing visions of who deserves recognition and legitimacy — and recasts great-power competition from power projection to power reception: the same logics are received differently depending on where a group sits in the dual hierarchy. 理论论文提出全球秩序逻辑——美国的自由个人主义与中国的国家本位多元主义两套竞争性秩序观——并将大国竞争从“权力投射”重新框定为“权力接收”：同一套逻辑，因群体在双重等级中的位置不同而被差异化地接收。

Rising Powers & Recognition崛起大国与承认

How do rising "outsider" states seek recognition within a Western-centric hierarchy? Analyzing 354 top-leader foreign-policy speeches (1978–2021), I argue China's compliance with and contestation of the existing order stem from the same recognition dilemma: as an outsider expecting great-power recognition, China must balance — rather than choose between — development-oriented conformity (material acceptance) and nationalist assertion (symbolic recognition of sovereignty and equal standing). 崛起的“外来国”如何在西方中心等级中争取承认？通过分析 354 篇最高领导人外交政策讲话（1978–2021），我认为中国对既有秩序的遵从与争夺，源于同一个承认困境：作为期待大国承认的外来者，中国须在“发展导向的遵从”（物质接纳）与“民族主义主张”（对主权与平等地位的象征性承认）之间反复校准，而非线性取舍。

The framework travels to other rising outsiders — India, Turkey, Brazil — where material advancement unmatched by symbolic recognition turns states toward nationalist assertion, challenging the order from within rather than breaking away. 该框架进一步延伸至印度、土耳其、巴西等其他崛起外来国：当物质崛起未获得相应的象征承认，国家便转向民族主义主张，从内部而非决裂地挑战既有秩序。
